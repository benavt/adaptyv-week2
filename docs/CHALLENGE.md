# Challenge 02: conditional TNF-alpha binder

Source: [official challenge](https://proteinbase.com/competitions/anthropic-adaptyv-2026/challenges/tnf-alpha). Checked 2026-10-05. Recheck before submission.

Design against soluble trimeric TNF-alpha. Objectives include Human affinity,
Mouse cross-reactivity, and Human binding at pH 7.4 with release at pH 6.0.
The suggested functional epitope lies between adjacent protomers. Select actual
construct sequences, structures, chain copies and hotspot numbering explicitly.

The submission closes `2026-10-11T23:59:00-12:00` (AoE), equivalent to
`2026-10-12T07:59:00-04:00` in New York. Ranked CSVs require `name`, `sequence`,
and `molecule_class`. Single-chain proteins are 10–250 residues. Track 1 allows
up to 40 designs; Tracks 2/3 allow up to 20. Confirm the participant track.

Eligibility includes de novo/zero-shot design; modifying an existing binder is
not an eligible design route. Researcher review is required before submission.
Keep methods, predicted measurements and source provenance distinct from
experimental evidence. The empty ranked template is in submissions/.
