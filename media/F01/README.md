This regenerated zoomed animation follows one linked binder from each of 23 native AFSample3 models of F01, a domain-linker-domain design, against a human TNF-alpha trimer. It illustrates configurations in which the N-terminal domain, both domains, or the C-terminal domain approach their corresponding parent-defined pockets. The trimer and camera stay fixed after target alignment. The source palette is retained: gray TNF, cyan N-terminal domain, green GS linker and hot pink C-terminal domain.

The anchor models were selected from a 1,000-model distance analysis: 900 native single-binder models and 100 retained three-binder models, with one linked binder analyzed per point. The 23 movie anchors comprise 21 single-binder models and two three-binder models; only one linked binder is displayed at each anchor. Each distance is the mean, over a fixed parent-defined TNF pocket, of the minimum heavy-atom distance to its corresponding domain; linker atoms are excluded. The pockets contain 22 and 24 TNF residues, respectively, defined by parent contacts within 5 Å.

The 15.73-second movie has 236 logical frames at 15 fps. Smooth transitions interpolate between the 23 sampled structures for visualization; they are not additional predictions or a molecular-dynamics trajectory. The path inset reports native anchor distances and interpolated path coordinates. This visualization supplements the selected F01 structures; it does not replace the exact-sequence scoring results.
